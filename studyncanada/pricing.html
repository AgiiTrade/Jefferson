Membership FAQ

How does billing work?

You're billed monthly via Stripe (annual option available at 20% off). Cancel anytime — you keep access until the end of your billing period.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

What if I just want one-time help with my visa?

Use the one-time service packages above. The Visa Application Package ($499) is the most popular standalone option.

Do you accept students from outside India / South Asia?

Yes — we work with international students from any country applying to Canadian universities and colleges.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your application checklist and documents stay in your account on the Free plan. Nothing is deleted.

Is there a student discount?

The Free plan is genuinely useful on its own. Annual Pro saves 20% (≈ $23/mo). Reach out via contact form for institutional/group rates.
