Insurance · Siebel → Salesforce

Atlantic Insurance Phase 1

A mid-market insurer with a 15-year-old Siebel CRM estate, a regulator-mandated digital-channel roadmap, and a stalled prior modernization attempt.

The challenge

Three previous consultant teams had scoped the migration. None had been able to extract the underwriting and claims business rules buried in Siebel eScript and SIF configurations. Without those rules, no migration plan could survive contact with reality.

What Agii did

  • Ingested the full Siebel estate — eScript, SIF, integration objects, business components
  • Extracted 1,400+ business rules into a navigable catalog
  • Produced a phased Salesforce migration roadmap with prioritized work packages
  • Mapped every Siebel integration to its Salesforce or middleware equivalent
  • Delivered an audit-ready evidence package for the regulator

Outcome

6-week Phase 1 pilot completed. Engineering leadership approved a 12-month Phase 2 build on the back of the roadmap. Pilot delivered at a fraction of the prior consultant scope.

Banking · COBOL → Spring Boot

Regional bank core modernization

A regional bank running COBOL batch on a mainframe, facing escalating MIPS costs and a vanishing COBOL talent pool.

The challenge

The bank's overnight settlement batch was 850K lines of COBOL across 240 programs, with deep JCL orchestration and embedded DB2 SQL. Logic had drifted from documentation over two decades. A rewrite without parity guarantees was not acceptable to the regulator.

What Agii did

  • Parsed all 240 COBOL programs and the JCL graph
  • Extracted embedded DB2 SQL into a target PostgreSQL schema
  • Generated idiomatic Spring Boot services preserving program-graph order
  • Ran logic-parity tests across 18 months of replayed transactions
  • Produced AWS ECS Fargate deploy templates with VPC isolation

Outcome

Parity-tested target system delivered. Mainframe MIPS cost projected to drop 70% post-cutover. Regulator review file delivered as part of the pilot.

Pharmacy · AS/400 → AWS

National pharmacy chain

A national pharmacy operator running prescription, inventory, and dispensing workflows on an AS/400 (IBM i) platform — facing end-of-vendor-support and an aging RPG developer pool.

The challenge

The estate was 1.2M lines of RPG IV, CL, and DDS, with hundreds of physical files, complex display-file workflows, and tight integration with regulatory reporting systems. The team needed a path to AWS that didn't require rewriting from a blank page.

What Agii did

  • Parsed RPG IV, CL, and DDS — rebuilt the full program and file graph
  • Translated DDS physical files into PostgreSQL schemas
  • Generated a Spring Boot scaffold preserving the original module boundaries
  • Produced AWS ECS Fargate deployment templates
  • Mapped each regulatory reporting integration to its modern equivalent

Outcome

Worked national-pharmacy case study available on request. End-of-support risk addressed. Developer hiring pool shifted from shrinking (RPG) to abundant (Java / Spring).
