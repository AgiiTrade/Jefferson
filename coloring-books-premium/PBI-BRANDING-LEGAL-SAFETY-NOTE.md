# PBI Branding and Copyright Safety Note

## Brand created
- Brand mark: **PBI Creative Kids**
- Logo concept: original book + sparkle mark with custom PBI wordmark treatment.
- Files: `brand/pbi-logo.svg`, `brand/pbi-logo.png`, `brand/pbi-logo.pdf`.

## Practical IP safety checks performed
- Avoided copying the uploaded/reference image directly. The PBI logo is newly generated from basic geometric/vector elements.
- Avoided protected franchise names, licensed characters, celebrity names, and third-party logos in listing copy.
- Themes are generic/public-domain concepts: dinosaurs, ocean animals, space, puppies/kittens, trucks/cars, unicorns, princess castles, bugs/butterflies, alphabet animals, farm animals.
- Added copyright notice: `© 2026 PBI. All rights reserved.`

## Trademark note
A web search found existing uses of “PBI” in unrelated contexts and at least one historical/potential publishing-related mention. Using the exact acronym **PBI** is not guaranteed clear for trademark registration. For lowest risk on KDP, use **PBI Creative Kids** as the visible imprint/brand and avoid claiming exclusive trademark rights until a formal USPTO/CIPO trademark search is completed by counsel.

## Legal disclaimer
This is a practical publishing/IP risk review, not legal advice. Before scaling paid ads or filing trademarks, run formal clearance in USPTO/CIPO and consider registering the final brand/logo.
