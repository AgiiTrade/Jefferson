# Lead Magnet Spec — Free 5-Page Sampler PDF

## Purpose
Capture email addresses on the Etsy thank-you page (and optionally as a standalone opt-in on a landing page). Goal: turn one-time buyers into email list subscribers who receive the nurture sequence and repeat-purchase offers.

---

## Product Concept
**Name:** "The PBI Creative Kids Sampler — 5 Free Printable Coloring Pages"

**Value proposition:** One free page from each of 5 different books in the collection, bundled into a single PDF — giving the buyer a taste of the full range before they've committed to any one title.

**Delivery mechanic:** Include a note in the Etsy order confirmation message (automated via Etsy's Message to Buyers setting): "P.S. — Want 5 free bonus coloring pages? Reply 'FREEBIE' to this message and we'll send them right to you." This bypasses Etsy's restrictions on email capture while initiating a direct conversation that can transition to email.

**Alternative delivery:** Add a short URL or QR code to a landing page (e.g., pbiCreativeKids.com/free or a Linktree link) in the Etsy listing description footer: "Free 5-page sampler: [link]." The landing page collects email, delivers the PDF, and enrolls the subscriber in the nurture sequence.

---

## PDF Design Brief

### Format
- Size: 8.5 x 11 inches, portrait
- Pages: 7 total (cover + 5 coloring pages + CTA back page)
- Color mode: Grayscale (optimized for home printing)
- Resolution: 300 DPI

### Page-by-Page Spec

**Page 1 — Cover**
- Headline: "5 Free Printable Coloring Pages"
- Subhead: "A sampler from the PBI Creative Kids collection"
- Design note: Simple, clean cover with the PBI Creative Kids logo centered. Avoid heavy ink coverage — this will be printed by parents on home printers. Use white background with a single thin decorative border.
- Small line at bottom: "Print unlimited copies for personal and classroom use."

**Page 2 — From Dinosaur Adventures**
- Include 1 T-Rex or Triceratops page (bold, high-recognition dino)
- Small footer text: "From: Dinosaur Adventures — 50-page printable coloring book. Full collection at [ETSY_SHOP_URL]"

**Page 3 — From Magical Unicorns**
- Include 1 unicorn-with-rainbow page (the most pinnable, shareable design)
- Small footer: "From: Magical Unicorns — 50-page printable coloring book. Full collection at [ETSY_SHOP_URL]"

**Page 4 — From ABC Animal Alphabet**
- Include 1 letter page (recommend: A for Alligator or L for Lion — high visual impact)
- Small footer: "From: ABC Animal Alphabet — 50-page printable coloring book. Full collection at [ETSY_SHOP_URL]"

**Page 5 — From Space Adventures**
- Include 1 rocket-launch or solar system page
- Small footer: "From: Space Adventures — 50-page printable coloring book. Full collection at [ETSY_SHOP_URL]"

**Page 6 — From Under the Sea**
- Include 1 octopus or sea turtle page (high visual detail, rewarding to color)
- Small footer: "From: Under the Sea — 50-page printable coloring book. Full collection at [ETSY_SHOP_URL]"

**Page 7 — Back Cover / CTA**
- Headline: "Enjoyed these pages?"
- Body: "Get the full 50-page collection for any of these titles — just $4.99 each. Instant digital download on Etsy."
- List all 10 book titles with short descriptions (1 line each)
- CTA button mockup: "Shop the full collection → [ETSY_SHOP_URL]"
- Bottom line: "© 2026 PBI Creative Kids. For personal and classroom use only."

## Projected Performance
- Etsy converts roughly 1–3% of visitors to buyers; expect 15–30% of buyers to opt in to the freebie
- Email nurture sequence target: 15–25% of subscribers purchase a second title within 30 days
- At 10 sales/week: ~2–3 email opt-ins/week, ~1 additional sale per week from nurture sequence
