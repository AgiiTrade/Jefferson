## Rotation Notes

- Replace ETSY_URL_* placeholders with actual Etsy listing URLs before scheduling
- Asset paths are relative to `individual-products/<slug>/listing-images/`
- Rotate asset variety each cycle: Week 1 leans on covers, Week 2 on mockups, Week 3 on contact sheets
- Add emojis to X posts selectively — 1–2 max per post performs best on X
- IG posts should always end with 5–8 hashtags in the caption
- FB posts perform better with a direct URL in the post body rather than "link in bio"
