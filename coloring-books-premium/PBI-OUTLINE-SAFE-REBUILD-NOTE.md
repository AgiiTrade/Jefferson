# PBI Outline-Safe Interior Rebuild

All 10 KDP bundle interiors were rebuilt on 2026-05-21 after user noticed weak/missing outlines on every second page. The new interiors use a higher threshold and slight stroke thickening to preserve faint line art in PDF/KDP viewers. Bundles were rebuilt with the new `interior.pdf` files plus `outline-safe-contact-sheet.jpg` for review.
